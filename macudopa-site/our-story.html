Our story

Innovative. Optimistic. Empowering.

A brand that brings together two usually distant worlds, natural medicine and predictable performance, so people with Parkinson's can find steady hope.

Our values

Three ideas that shape everything we make.

01

Innovative

No two Parkinson's patients are the same. We create more precise, consistent and personalised approaches to supporting their symptoms, dose after dose.

02

Optimistic

Many people come to us feeling low or frustrated. Our role is to help shift that towards feeling more steady, more able, and more like who they were before.

03

Empowering

We give people with Parkinson's the products, knowledge and confidence to better manage their symptoms and become their own advocate.

How it started

A life, and a reason.

MacuDopa was born from the experience of someone we love. A successful career, a diagnosis with Parkinson's at 48, and a search for a natural alternative that had simply not existed. That gap, between a life well lived and the tools available, is what propelled us to create MacuDopa.

Every movement, every quiet moment of stillness, every tremor we have watched taught us what a support product must do: be precise, be gentle, and stay with you. We consider each detail without losing sight of the whole.

Rooted in nature

Mucuna, made to measure.

Practitioner guided

Support that walks with you.

We know how unpredictable Parkinson's can be. No two patients, no two days, are the same. Which is why we are with you throughout your journey, with guides, advice and practitioners on hand when you need to talk. We work with you to understand MacuDopa, and find your rhythm and dosage.
