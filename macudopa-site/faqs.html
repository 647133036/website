Frequently asked questions

The answers to what people ask most often about MacuDopa, Mucuna and finding your timing. If you need more, our practitioners are on hand.

Mucuna pruriens, also known as the velvet bean, is a plant that has been used for millennia. It naturally contains L-DOPA, the compound your body converts into dopamine. Because it is a whole, natural ingredient, it offers a gentler and more sustainable way to support the dopamine system, with fewer of the intense side effects that can come with isolated synthetic forms.

Take your next dose of MacuDopa just before the last one fades. This helps keep dopamine steady and avoids 'off' periods. The Day formula is a targeted formula to help you stay focused for what's ahead, and the Night formula is a calming blend to support relaxation, restful sleep and overnight recovery. Dosing is personal, and our practitioners help you find your rhythm.

Both work by the same principle: replacing lost dopamine. The difference is the source and the way it is delivered. MacuDopa uses Mucuna made to measure, with HPLC standardisation so every capsule is precisely and predictably the same. It is a whole ingredient, not stripped or refined, which tends to make it gentler, with a more natural and sustained effect.

MacuDopa is practitioner guided. No two patients, no two days, are the same, and we are with you throughout your journey. Our guides, advice and practitioners are on hand when you need to talk. We work with you to understand MacuDopa and find your rhythm and dosage. Write to us at hello@macudopa.com.

'Off' periods are times when symptoms such as stiffness, slowness, tremor, anxiety or brain fog return or worsen. They often happen when dopamine levels drop, not because Parkinson's has suddenly progressed, but because support has worn off, timing has shifted, or the body is under extra demand from stress, poor sleep, food or illness. Taking your next dose just before the last one fades helps keep those 'off' periods fewer and less intense.
